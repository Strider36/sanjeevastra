Help Desk

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries regarding security, data backups, and OCR reliability.

Is my health data secure on Sanjeev Astra?
Absolutely. Your sensitive credentials and personal profiles are encrypted. We hash accounts via bcrypt algorithms and structure user document uploads under highly secure private token authorizations.
How reliable is the AI OCR scanner?
Our scanner leverages Tesseract.js to scan medicine boxes and handwritten doctor prescriptions. Since readability varies based on camera angles, lighting, and doctor penmanship, we display editable forms so you can correct details before saving.
Does the platform provide actual diagnoses?
No. Sanjeev Astra and its AI assistant provide general wellness pointers, preliminary care level suggestions, and emergency coordination. Responses are strictly educational guidance and not a replacement for advice from certified clinical professionals.
